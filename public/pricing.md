# OutcomePay pricing

OutcomePay is a **free sandbox prototype** built for the PayPal AI Hackathon 2026. There are no paid tiers, no subscriptions, and no production payments.

## Tiers

### Sandbox (the only tier)

- Price: **$0**
- Everything is free to try: publishing outcome contracts, provider bids, team formation, deliveries, verification, approvals, and settlement.
- Payments run on the **PayPal sandbox** — no real money moves. Settlement captures are test transactions against sandbox accounts.
- Outcome budgets are capped at **$10,000** per contract (a prototype guardrail, not a pricing limit).
- Provider allocations shown after settlement are a **simulated internal ledger**, not PayPal Payouts and not real disbursements.
- Rate guidance: ~60 requests/minute per client.

## Non-goals

- No production/commercial offering exists today. If that changes, this page changes first.
- OutcomePay is not a legal escrow service.
